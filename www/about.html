Origin

A Standard Set in Service

CS Executive Services, LLC was founded by Corey Sheldon, a United States Marine Corps veteran long established in the Washington, DC region. The discipline, precision, and mission-orientation developed through military service are not values adopted for professional effect — they are the habits of a life, and they govern every practice the firm undertakes.

The firm is deliberately small and principal-led. Clients engage a person, not an organisation. The individual who receives your enquiry is the individual who conducts the work — there is no delegation, no handoff, no dilution of attention at the moment it is most required.

That arrangement is a choice, not a limitation. It is the means by which the firm holds itself to the standard it has set.

Privacy as a Condition of Practice

Confidentiality is not a feature of the firm's offering. It is the condition under which the firm operates at all. No client names, engagement details, or identifying information appear in any public-facing material, at any time. Discretion is observed from the first correspondence — and maintained indefinitely thereafter.

This commitment extends across all four practice areas. Whether the engagement concerns transportation, a vehicle, a brand, or a security posture, the terms are private and the relationship is held accordingly.

Washington, DC and Beyond

The firm is based in Arlington County, Virginia, 15 minutes from the capital. The chauffeur practice serves Reagan National, Dulles International, and Baltimore-Washington International airports, as well as corporate and residential destinations throughout the greater metropolitan area.

For engagements requiring travel beyond the region, arrangements may be made by correspondence.
