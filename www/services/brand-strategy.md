## Counsel, Not Consulting by Committee.

Brand strategy at the executive level is neither a design exercise nor a
marketing function. It is the considered articulation of what an
organisation — or an individual — stands for, how that is expressed in
practice, and why it warrants the trust of those whose regard it seeks.

The firm works with founders, senior executives, and organisations at
points of inflection: building an identity, repositioning an established
one, managing the consequences of reputational difficulty, or preparing
for a material transition. The work is conducted privately, without
formula, and without staff. Every engagement is a conversation between
principal and client.

### What the Work Involves

An engagement ordinarily begins with an honest assessment of the present
positioning — its strengths, its inconsistencies, and the distance
between what is intended and what is in fact received. From that
foundation the firm advises on identity, message, visual and verbal
expression, and the underlying logic by which a brand ought to be
applied.

The firm also advises on narrative — the account an organisation gives
of itself, the contexts in which that account is offered, and the
discipline required to sustain it without rigidity. This is among the
most frequently underestimated dimensions of brand strategy, and the
least often attended to with the care it deserves.

### Discretion as Standard

The nature of this work frequently involves sensitive strategic
information. No details of any engagement — its existence, its scope, or
its conclusions — are discussed or referenced outside the engagement
itself. All client relationships are held in complete confidence.
