About the Firm

Founded on Service.
Sustained by Discretion.

CS Executive Services was established by a United States Marine Corps veteran in Arlington County, Virginia. The discipline, attention to detail, and mission-orientation cultivated in military service inform every aspect of the firm's practice.

The firm serves those to whom a high standard of professional conduct is simply assumed — and who understand that what finally distinguishes a private service provider is not competence alone, but the trust competence earns.

All four practice areas are conducted under a single commitment: complete confidentiality in every engagement, without exception.
