# CS Executive Services, LLC — Brand Standards

> *This document is the canonical reference for all client-facing copy, digital presence,
> printed materials, and correspondence produced under the CS Executive Services, LLC name.
> It governs all four practice areas without exception.*

---

## I. The Register

All copy written on behalf of CS Executive Services, LLC is composed in **Old World English
register** — the language of long-established private practice, not of the marketplace.

The standard is formal, measured, and unhurried. Sentences are complete. Paragraphs breathe.
The reader is never rushed, never sold to, never addressed as a consumer.

**The guiding principle:** the firm speaks as a trusted private advisor would — with authority,
economy, and complete discretion.

### What this sounds like

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|
| *We would be pleased to arrange your transportation.* | *Book your luxury ride today!* |
| *The firm's work is conducted with complete confidentiality.* | *Your privacy is our #1 priority!* |
| *We invite you to correspond at your convenience.* | *Get a free quote — fast!* |
| *Each engagement is arranged privately and individually.* | *Check out our pricing packages below.* |
| *A member of the firm will be in contact by your preferred means.* | *We'll reach out ASAP!* |

### What to avoid

- Exclamation marks in client-facing copy
- Superlatives: *world-class, best-in-class, premier, top-rated, #1*
- Urgency language: *today, now, fast, instant, limited time*
- American marketing idiom: *leverage, solutions, cutting-edge, seamless*
- Casual register or contractions in formal contexts
- Publishing rates, figures, or pricing tiers of any kind

## III. Practice Areas

The register and rates policy apply uniformly across all four disciplines.
The following notes describe how the register is expressed within each area's specific context.

### Executive Chauffeur Transportation

Private ground transportation in the Washington, DC metropolitan area and beyond.
Engagements include airport transfers, corporate meetings, full-day retainers, evening
and event service, estate and winery visits, and wedding parties.

Copy describes the character and scope of the service — the quality of the vehicle,
the discretion of the chauffeur, the standard of in-cabin provision. It does not itemise
amenities as a features list or suggest a tariff by implication. The word *package* is avoided.

Fleet, vehicle class, and specific amenities are confirmed in private correspondence.

### Automotive Detailing

Premium detailing conducted with care and precision. Each vehicle is treated individually;
no standard formula is applied.

Copy speaks to the quality of craft, the discretion of the process, and the respect
afforded to the client's property. It does not enumerate service tiers or comparative
grades. There is no *basic*, *standard*, or *premium* on the public-facing side.

### Brand Strategy & Positioning

Executive-level consulting in brand identity, positioning, and strategic communication.
Clients are typically founders, senior executives, and organisations at an inflection point.

Copy conveys the depth of the firm's approach and its capacity for discretion. It does not
publish case studies, name clients, or enumerate deliverables. The nature of the engagement
is described; its scope is agreed privately.

### IT Security

Privacy-first security consulting for organisations that take discretion seriously.
Work includes security assessment, policy development, risk framing, and advisory retainer.

Copy reflects the gravity and rigour appropriate to the discipline. It does not publish
methodologies, toolchains, or certifications in promotional language. The firm's approach
is described in terms of posture and values, not credentials and features.

## IV. Visual Identity

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#0a1628` | Primary background, dominant dark surfaces |
| Navy-secondary | `#13233d` | Cards, overlays, secondary panels |
| Amber | `#e8a124` | Accent only — rule lines, hover states, key callouts |
| Slate | `#3a4a5a` | Supporting dark text on light surfaces |
| Light gray | `#eef1f4` | Light background sections |
| Mid gray | `#b8c2cc` | Secondary text, captions, metadata |
| White | `#ffffff` | Body text on dark; clean light surfaces |

**Amber is an accent, not a theme colour.** It appears sparingly — a horizontal rule,
a hover underline, a single callout. It loses its authority if overused.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | Cormorant Garamond | Italic for pull quotes and descriptive passages |
| Body | DM Sans | Regular weight; generous line height |
| Labels / navigation | Montserrat | Uppercase, wide tracking, small size |

## V. Enquiry and Correspondence Tone

Initial outbound correspondence from the firm — whether by email, telephone, or
returned enquiry — mirrors the register above. It is warm without being familiar,
prompt without communicating haste, and specific without being transactional.

The client's preferred method of response, as indicated on the enquiry form, is honoured.
If no preference is given, correspondence by email is the default first contact.

---

## VI. Confidentiality

The firm treats all enquiries, engagements, and client relationships with complete
confidentiality. This commitment is not a marketing statement; it is a condition of practice.

No client names, engagements, or identifying details appear in any public-facing material
without explicit written consent.

### Exception: Time References

Time durations are expressed in numerals, not words or archaic forms.

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|
| *15 minutes from the capital* | *fifteen minutes from the capital* |
| *within 30 minutes* | *within half an hour / within a half-hour* |
| *a 2-hour engagement* | *a two-hour engagement / a two-hour retainer* |

This preserves readability and modern convention without compromising the register.
