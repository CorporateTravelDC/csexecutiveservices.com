## Register

**Old World English throughout.** Formal, measured, unhurried. The language of a private members'
club, a Savile Row tailor, or a long-established London solicitor. No American marketing idiom.
No superlatives. No "world-class" or "best-in-class." No exclamation marks. No urgency.
Sentences are complete. Paragraphs breathe.

Example wrong: "Book your luxury ride today!"
Example right:  "We would be pleased to arrange your transportation at your convenience."

## Rates & Pricing

**None published. Ever.** This is a private, by-arrangement firm. All engagements are
discussed privately and quoted individually. The site never mentions prices, hourly rates,
packages, or tiers with figures. Services are described by their character and scope only.

The call to action on every service page is a discreet invitation to correspond — not
a booking widget, not a price list, not a "Get a Quote" button.

## Chauffeur Service — Character Description (no rates)

Private executive transportation throughout the Washington DC metropolitan area and beyond.
Engagements include airport transfers (Reagan National, Dulles, BWI), corporate meetings,
full-day retainers, evening and event service, winery and estate visits, and wedding parties.

Fleet: late-model, immaculately maintained vehicles. Amenities vary by engagement and are
confirmed at time of arrangement — WiFi, still water, charging, and in-cabin entertainment
are available as standard. Complimentary refreshment service and extended-stop arrangements
are accommodated upon request.

All arrangements are private and confirmed in correspondence. There is no published tariff.
